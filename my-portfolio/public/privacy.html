Privacy

This is a personal portfolio. It is built to collect as little as possible.

What this site does not do

It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. It has no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking pixels. Fonts, images and videos are all served from this site, so opening a page sends nothing to any third party.

What the hosting provider sees

The site is hosted on Google Firebase Hosting. Like any web host, it receives your IP address and browser details in order to deliver the pages, and keeps standard server logs. I do not have access to those logs in a form that identifies visitors, and I do not use them.

Links and contact

Project tiles link to other websites, which have their own privacy practices. If you email or call me, I receive whatever you choose to send and use it only to reply.
